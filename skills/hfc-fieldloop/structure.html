After drop-in (project root)

The survey project gains an hfc/ product tree: checks/, code/, config/, registry/, output/, report/, fixes/, structure.html, and project.yaml. Raw data stays under data/raw/; accepted fixes write *_resolved siblings. Open hfc/structure.html in a browser and confirm Continue before the full build. Gone from the skill: eval/, verify/demo harness, SimUser.
